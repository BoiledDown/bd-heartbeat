Canary Black Bean Soup

Serves 6 · Prep 15 minutes · Cook 35 minutes

What you need:

2 tablespoons olive oil
1 large yellow onion, diced
1 red bell pepper, diced
3 cloves garlic, minced
2 teaspoons ground cumin
1 teaspoon smoked paprika
3 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
4 cups vegetable broth
1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
1 teaspoon salt
Juice of 1 lime
Sour cream and cilantro, for topping

How to make it:

Start by warming the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and bell pepper and cook for about 6 minutes until soft.

Next, stir in the garlic, cumin, and smoked paprika and cook for 1 minute.

Add the black beans, broth, tomatoes, and salt. Bring everything to a boil, then reduce to a simmer for 25 minutes.

Blend about half the soup with an immersion blender (or transfer half to a blender and return it) so it's thick but still chunky.

Finally, stir in the lime juice, taste for salt, and serve topped with sour cream and cilantro.
