Heartbeat Lemon Garlic Chicken

Prep 10 min · Cook 25 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley

Instructions

  1. Pat the chicken dry and season both sides with salt, pepper, and oregano.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken 5 to 6 minutes per side until golden, then move it to a plate.
  3. Lower the heat to medium. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Stir in the broth, lemon juice, and lemon zest, scraping up the browned bits. Simmer 3 minutes.
  5. Return the chicken to the pan and simmer 8 to 10 minutes until it reaches 165°F inside.
  6. Swirl in the butter until melted, sprinkle with parsley, and serve.
